Terms, Privacy & Refund Policy

Booking and payment

Bookings are confirmed only after full payment through Chapa (Telebirr, CBE Birr, cards and other methods). When you start a booking we hold the room for 10 minutes; if payment is not completed in that time the room is released. Prices are in Ethiopian Birr (ETB) per night.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in and check-out times are shown on the booking page and in your confirmation. Please bring a valid ID.

Cancellation and refunds

If you cancel at least 24 hours before check-in you receive a refund. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before check-in, and no-shows, are not refunded. To cancel, call +251 921 414 245 with your booking reference.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, phone number and stay details only to manage your booking and contact you about it. Payments are processed by Chapa; we do not see or store your card or wallet details. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, except as required by law.
